# Intake: builder habit dashboard

## Mode

`full-project`

## Project profile

Primary: `dashboard-admin-bi`

## Blocking questions

None for this fixture. Persistence and account requirements are treated as reversible assumptions.

## Reversible assumptions

- The first version is local-first.
- Habit data stays on the device unless the operator explicitly asks for accounts.
- The product tone is private, calm, and non-competitive.

## Non-goals

- Social sharing.
- Leaderboards.
- Third-party analytics.
- Automatic health advice.

## Anti-genericity constraints

- Must avoid generic SaaS cards unless every card maps to a habit workflow.
- Must not turn the app into a productivity dashboard clone.
- Must express habit data as sensitive personal routine data.
